Bhawesh Joshi is a creative developer, digital designer and the founder of BuildStack. I design and build websites and interfaces, and I care as much about how something works as how it looks.

Philosophy
A website is a first conversation. It should be clear about who you are, easy to use, and memorable enough to be worth a second visit.
What I focus on
Modern websites, interface design and interactive experiences, with design and development handled together so decisions stay consistent from first sketch to final code.
How I solve problems
I start with the problem, not the template. I work out what the site needs to do, plan the structure, design it with purpose, then build and refine until it works well on every screen.
Interests
Interface design, interactive web experiences, and AI-inspired concepts. I like experimenting with new technology and finding ways to make the web more engaging.

Why work with me

Why work with me.

01

Design + development together

One person designs and builds, so the intent of a design isn't lost between a mockup and the finished code.

02

Responsive-first thinking

Layouts are planned for small screens first and then expanded, so phones get a designed experience, not a shrunken desktop page.

03

Clean, thoughtful interfaces

Clear hierarchy, readable type and fewer, better-chosen elements, so visitors always know where to look and what to do.

04

Attention to detail

Spacing, alignment, hover states and focus states get as much care as the big layout decisions.

05

Interactive experiences

Motion and interaction are used where they help people understand or enjoy a page, and switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

06

Performance & usability

Lightweight code and accessible markup, so pages load quickly and can be used with a keyboard as well as a mouse or touch screen.

Process

A clear path from idea to launch.

Six steps, no surprises. You know what happens next at every stage.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We talk through your goals, your audience and what the site needs to achieve. You share content, references and any existing site.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    I plan the structure, key pages and visitor journey, and agree the scope with you before design starts.

  3. 03

    Design

    Wireframes first, then visual design: layout, typography, colour and interaction details, shared with you for review.

  4. 04

    Development

    The approved design is built as a responsive, accessible website with clean, maintainable code.

  5. 05

    Testing & Optimization

    Checks across screen sizes and browsers, plus performance, accessibility and form testing.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Final checks, going live, and a handover so you know how to look after the site.
